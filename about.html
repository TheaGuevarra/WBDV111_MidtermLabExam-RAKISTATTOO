Our Story

Where It All Began

Founded in 2017, Rakistattoo began with a simple foundation: a deep love for the craft and a commitment to every person who walked through our doors. We didn't start with flashy advertisements; we started with a pair of steady hands and the smiles of our first satisfied neighbors. To us, you aren't just a client — you are a collaborator. Every line we ink and every piercing we place is handled with a gentle touch and a focus on your comfort. We believe that an artful tattoo is a partnership of trust, and we pour our heart into ensuring your experience is as memorable as the art you take home.

The Rakistattoo Promise

Trust Earned, Not Given

Rakistattoo has been dedicated to the art of transformation. What began as a journey fueled by raw skill and the support of our local community has grown into a sanctuary for those seeking thoughtful tattoos and piercings. We understand that trust is earned, not given. That's why we prioritize a safe, welcoming environment where your vision is respected and your well-being is our first priority. From the first sketch to the final healing steps, we are with you every inch of the way.

About Rakistattoo

A Studio That Feels Like Home

Established in 2017, Rakistattoo was built on the word-of-mouth of happy friends and a passion for honest artistry. Our studio is a space where your ideas become timeless art and your safety is never a secondary thought. We lean on years of dedication to provide a tattooing and piercing experience that feels like home. Whether it's your first piece or your twentieth, we put our best into every detail to ensure you leave our studio with art you love and a memory you cherish.
